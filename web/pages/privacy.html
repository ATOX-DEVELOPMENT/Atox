Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Atox collects, why, and what choices you have. In short: your projects, files and chats stay on your computer, we collect very little, and we never sell your data.

Who is responsible

Atox is operated by Abdumalik Fattaev, an individual based in Uzbekistan ("we", "us"), who is the controller of the data described here. Contact: malikfattaevx@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your computer

Your projects, files, chats, drafts and settings are stored locally on your computer. We do not receive them. Your agents use your own subscription or API key and talk to their provider (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) directly, under that provider's privacy policy.

What we collect

Anonymous usage statistics

The app sends anonymous statistics to our own server so we know how many people use Atox: an install event once, then one event per day, week and month the app is open. Each event carries only the app version, the operating system name and a random number for the current launch of the app. The server looks up the country from the IP address and keeps only the country; the address itself is not stored. You can turn this off in Settings → General.

Updates and downloads

The app checks atox.dev for new versions and downloads them from it. These are normal web requests, including your IP address, kept only in the server logs described below.

Shared canvases

When you share a canvas, its screens, images and fonts are uploaded to share.atox.dev so that anyone with the link can view them. They stay there until you stop sharing, which deletes them. Nothing is uploaded unless you share.

Server logs and email

Our servers and hosting provider keep standard technical logs, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting, for up to 30 days. If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you.

Why we use it

  • To provide what you asked for: updates and shared canvases.
  • For our legitimate interests: security, preventing abuse and understanding how many people use Atox.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not train AI models on it.

Who we share it with

Only with services that help us run Atox, and only as needed:

  • Railway — hosting of our website and servers;
  • GitHub — storage of app releases.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of others. If Atox is transferred to another owner, your data moves with it under this policy, and we will tell you.

International transfers

These services may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the standard contractual clauses of those services.

How long we keep it

  • shared canvases: until you stop sharing;
  • server logs: up to 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access is limited to what is needed to run Atox. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Atox is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

This website

atox.dev uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
